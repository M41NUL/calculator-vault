Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

Calculator Vault ("the App") is built around a single principle: your private files stay private, and stay on your device. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what happens (and what does not happen) to your data when you use the App. We are strictly committed to offline-first security and to minimizing what we collect to the absolute minimum required for the App to function.

2. Data Collection & Handling

The App does not harvest, sell, transmit, or otherwise share your photos, videos, notes, or documents to any developer-owned server. All vault content is encrypted and stored locally on your device by default.

  • We do not operate a remote database of user files.
  • We do not scan, analyze, or index the content of your vault.
  • We do not sell or share data with advertisers or third parties.

3. Google Drive API Access & Permissions

The optional cloud backup feature uses the Google Drive API under the following OAuth scopes:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

These scopes are used solely to store and restore encrypted vault backup snapshots that you initiate. The App can only access files it creates itself — it cannot view, list, or modify any other file in your Google Drive account.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Backup data remains encrypted before it ever leaves your device, and only you hold the key to decrypt it.

4. Local Storage & PIN Security

Your master PIN is never stored in plain text. Only a one-way cryptographic hash of your PIN, along with optional biometric unlock configuration, is stored strictly within Android's local DataStore on your device. This data is sandboxed to the App and is not accessible to us or to any other application.

5. Developer Contact

For any privacy inquiries, data questions, or concerns about this policy, reach the developer directly at devmainulislam@gmail.com.
